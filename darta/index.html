About

Darta is a native iPhone app for scoring 301 and 501. Enter each dart with a board-shaped picker or a numeric keypad, keep personal stats on your device, and practice against on-device bots under the same rules as human play.

The app is fully offline. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, and no ads in the current version. It requires iOS 26 or later.
