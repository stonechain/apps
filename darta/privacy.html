Overview

This privacy policy describes how the Darta iPhone app (“Darta”), published by StoneChain, handles information. Darta is designed to work offline and keep your match data on your device.

Data stored on your device

Darta stores the following on your iPhone only:

  • Player first name and optional second name
  • In-progress and completed match history
  • Personal stats derived from completed matches
  • App settings (for example scoring UI, announcer, appearance)

There are no accounts, no cloud sync, and no export features in the current version. StoneChain does not receive the data listed above.

Analytics and crash reporting

Darta does not include product analytics. The current version does not send crash reports to StoneChain or to any third party.

Speech and audio

When the announcer is on, Darta uses on-device speech synthesis provided by iOS. Spoken lines and scores are not uploaded. Audio follows the device silent switch and mixes with other audio according to system settings.

Advertising and purchases

The current version of Darta has no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Children

Darta is not directed at children and is not offered in Apple’s Kids category.

Deleting your data

Because Darta stores data only on your device, deleting the app removes that data from the device (subject to any backups you make with Apple’s system tools).

Contact

For privacy questions, contact StoneChain at darta@stonechain.com.
