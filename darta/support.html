How we can help

Darta is an iPhone scoring app for 301 and 501. It does not require an account. Match history, stats, and settings stay on your phone.

For questions, feedback, or problems with the app, email StoneChain at darta@stonechain.com.

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what you were doing when the issue happened. That helps us respond faster.

For how Darta handles information, see the Privacy Policy.
